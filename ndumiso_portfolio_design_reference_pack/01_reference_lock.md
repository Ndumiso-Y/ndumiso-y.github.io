## Authority and scope

The reference image is the **single visual authority for the approved desktop hero concept**. This approval does **not** claim that the entire one-page site is designed, nor does it authorise animation, forms changes, new content, dependencies, production deployment, or removal of existing functionality. The other six sections should follow this accepted design language but need their own section-specific design review. The image is a design target, not a flattened webpage asset.

## Approved characteristics (preserve)

1. **Editorial, portrait-led composition:** dominant two-line `NDUMISO / YEDWA.` headline left; large personal portrait right; clear secondary label `WEB DESIGNER & FRONT-END DEVELOPER`.
2. **Minimalist split colour treatment:** generous off-white/neutral canvas on left and a restrained, largely flat blue-teal field on the right. Avoid generic luminous gradients, abstract arcs, superimposed geometric decoration, unnecessary glow and ornamental UI.
3. **Subtle identity watermark:** the *actual existing* `ndu-icon` artwork, softly integrated behind the portrait, not a numeral `02`, a newly drawn face or other motif.
4. **Floating transparent glass-pill navigation:** compact and translucent, absorbs background colour behind it; uses actual existing icon/logo and preserves site section links.
5. **Image-led four-card row:** Websites, Graphics & Branding, Social Media Management, Learning Journey. The screenshot establishes relative card scale, treatment, overlay and typography. Final images must derive from existing permitted portfolio assets, not from stock/artificial examples.
6. **True translucent skills bar:** restrained pill-shaped strip showing existing skills/tools; visually glass-like rather than solid white. Keep all 11 existing skill entries in the site; the narrow bar is a curated preview, not a replacement for full Skills & Tools content.
7. **Authentic QR e-card panel:** preserved existing QR graphic and working e-card destination; visually secondary to name/portrait.
8. **Strong legibility and negative space:** subtle use of teal, restrained borders/shadows, deliberate spacing, and accessible controls.
